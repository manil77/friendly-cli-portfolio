Privacy notice

Last updated 28 September 2026 · Controller: Manil Maharjan, Nepal · manil.maharjan07@gmail.com

This is my personal portfolio. I want to know which of my work people find interesting so I can follow up on opportunities. I also want to respect your privacy. There are no ads, no third-party trackers, no session recordings, and I never sell or share data.

Your current choice: …

1. Always on: anonymous statistics

Every page view is counted without cookies or any storage in your browser. I record the page, the website that linked to it (domain only), your country, and whether you're on a phone, tablet or desktop. To count unique visitors per day, your IP address and browser type are combined with a random value that's deleted every day. The result can't be linked to you, and your IP address is never stored.

2. Only if you click "Allow": your visit

  • A random visitor ID in your browser's local storage, which links your visits together.
  • Your approximate location (city/region level) from your IP address.
  • The name of the network or company you browse from, looked up from your IP address via ipinfo.io. The IP address itself is not stored.
  • Pages viewed, projects clicked, terminal commands typed, time spent, and résumé downloads.

I use this to see which work resonates and to recognise returning visitors and companies, so I can reach out about relevant opportunities. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); Nepal Individual Privacy Act 2075). You can withdraw it any time with the button above. Withdrawing deletes this detailed data straight away.

3. Personal links

If I sent you a link made just for you, opening it records the time and your country so I know it was opened. Nothing more is recorded unless you click "Allow".

4. The contact form

If you send a message, I store your name, email, company (optional) and message, only so I can reply. If you allowed tracking, the message is linked to your visit history. Legal basis: your consent, given with the checkbox on the form.

6. How long it's kept

  • Anonymous statistics: 13 months.
  • Detailed visit data: 12 months after your last visit, or until you withdraw consent.
  • Contact messages: until the conversation is over, or at most 24 months. You can ask me to delete them sooner.

7. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion. You can also object or complain to your data protection authority. Email me at manil.maharjan07@gmail.com. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, I treat it as "No thanks" automatically. For US residents: I do not sell or share personal information for advertising.
